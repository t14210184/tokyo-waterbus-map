# Image & Content Policy: Tokyo Waterbus Atlas

> **Version**: `v1.1.0-RC.3.22`  
> **Status**: Content Sourcing & Intellectual Property Guidelines

---

## 📸 Image Sourcing & Rights Rules

1. **No Unauthorized Social Media Harvesting**: Images must NEVER be copied, scraped, or rehosted from private social media accounts, Instagram, Twitter/X, or personal blogs without explicit written licensing.
2. **Approved Sourcing Channels**:
   - Official operator media assets with explicit permission.
   - Wikimedia Commons assets under CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, or Public Domain licenses.
   - Original contributor photos submitted with signed terms of use.
3. **Mandatory Photo Metadata**: Every image displayed in pier cards or guides must store structured metadata:
   - `author`: Photographer name or entity.
   - `sourceUrl`: Canonical URL to original image source.
   - `license`: Explicit license type (e.g. `CC BY-SA 4.0`).
   - `date`: Photograph capture date.
   - `direction`: Landmark or exit orientation.
   - `alt`: Multilingual descriptive alt text.
4. **Wayfinding Over Decoration**: Photos must serve a functional purpose (e.g. identifying station exits, ticket counters, boarding gates, or step-free ramps) rather than merely decorating the screen.
5. **EXIF Privacy & Moderation**: Contributor photo uploads must undergo EXIF location data stripping for privacy, automated moderation, and a clear takedown request flow.
