# Product Vision: Tokyo Waterbus Atlas

> **Phase**: 0 (`v1.1.0-RC.3.22`)  
> **Status**: Active Product Vision & Long-Term Strategy

---

## 🌊 Core Philosophy: Truthful Traveller-First Reference Tool

Tokyo Waterbus Atlas is designed to empower on-site travellers, trip planners, remote explorers, and users with accessibility needs by providing a transparent, trustworthy reference guide for navigating Tokyo by water.

Rather than presenting an engineering-heavy audit dashboard or pretending to track live vessels via unverified GPS/AIS data, Tokyo Waterbus Atlas prioritizes **operational truth**:

1. **Clear Gateway to Official Truth**: Direct, immediate links to same-day official status and timetables.
2. **Pier & Last-300m Guidance**: Helping travellers find waterbus piers from train station exits without stress.
3. **Transparent Data Trust Levels**: Explicit labelling so users instantly distinguish official notices, timetable references, and offline concept demos.
4. **Accessible Remote Exploration**: Allowing users worldwide to explore routes and piers before arriving in Tokyo.

---

## 👥 Target User Profiles

- **On-Site Travellers**: Standing at Asakusa or Odaiba Pier needing to verify whether boats are running today and where ticket counters are located.
- **Trip Planners**: Planning itineraries across Asakusa, Hamarikyu, Hinode, Toyosu, and Odaiba.
- **Accessibility & Special Needs Travellers**: Requiring step-free access, wheelchair ramp information, accessible toilet locations, and shelter availability.
- **Remote Explorers**: Studying Tokyo's geography and historical water routes.
